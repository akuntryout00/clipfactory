# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive; no harassment, discrimination or personal attacks; assume good faith; keep discussions about the work.

Violations can be reported privately to the maintainers via the contact in SECURITY.md. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and PRs that do not align with this code, and may ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.
